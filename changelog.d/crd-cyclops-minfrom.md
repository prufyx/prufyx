### Changed

- The cyclops CRD extractor target now starts at release 0.19 (was 0.16). The releases 0.16 to 0.18 ship an empty generated stub definition (cyclops-ctrl/config/crd/bases/_.yaml, no group, no names) next to the real one, which the scan cannot read as a definition and which blocked every pair that touched them. From 0.19 on the stub is gone and the scan is clean. A dry run of the derive loop on the 0.19 start gives 2 attestable pairs (0.19 to 0.20 and 0.20 to 0.21), 0 withheld; line attestation was already on for this target. Attested pairs appear only after the factory re-derives them and the data change passes the QA gate and data review.
