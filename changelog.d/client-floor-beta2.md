### Changed

- `v0.1.0-beta.2` is the first release that knowledge packages with listed changelog entries or pack level `v1alpha16` are published for. Once the oldest supported client is `v0.1.0-beta.2` and such a package is published, `v0.1.0-beta.0` and `v0.1.0-beta.1` refuse it as too new (exit `15`) and receive no further knowledge from the feed, including withdrawals and renewals. Upgrade to `v0.1.0-beta.2` or later.
