### Added (workflows)

- The knowledge publish workflow becomes a daily plan, approval and sign run: a keyless plan job builds the packet of merged changes and checks the approval gate, the sign job (environment `knowledge-release`) signs only what the plan describes, and the per-push trigger is removed. The live package is the newest live artifact across the content runs and the timestamp refresh runs; sign and deploy refuse when either family moved since the plan. The trusted marker authors are pinned numeric ids. Not active until the owner adds the required reviewer; a manual run is a dry run by default, and the pull request lands after the owner steps (see "Daily signing"). The publish self-check allows 30 hours for a merge to be published and checks the expiry caps. Needs the packet generator and the publisher guard subcommands from the pull requests it depends on.
